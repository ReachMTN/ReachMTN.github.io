# Purchase & Sales & Inventory Analytics Dashboard

Interactive portfolio demo with synthetic records.

## Verified U.S. product timeline and MSRP
- iPhone 18 Pro Max preorder: September 12, 2026; retail availability: September 18, 2026. MSRP: 256GB $1,299; 512GB $1,499; 1TB $1,899; 2TB $2,499.
- iPhone Duo preorder begins October 16, 2026; retail availability begins October 23, 2026. MSRP: 256GB $1,999; 512GB $2,199; 1TB $2,599; 2TB $3,199.

The demo models iPhone 18 Pro Max launch-day purchases at MSRP and target resale profit around $180 per unit. iPhone Duo rows on Oct 16 are preorders at MSRP, not fulfilled inventory or completed sales. All personal-looking transaction rows are synthetic portfolio examples, not verified real transactions. Target resale prices are assumptions, not guaranteed market prices.

Files: `dashboard.html` (interactive dashboard), `checkout_transactions_sample.csv` (sample dataset), `checkout_analytics.sql` (SQL summaries), `powerbi_build_guide.md` (Power BI build notes).
