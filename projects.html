Purchase & Sales & Inventory Analytics

This project analyzes purchase activity, target resale revenue, gross profit, and inventory status using Power BI-style reporting and SQL. The dashboard compares product models, storage capacities, colors, acquisition costs, and estimated resale margins while organizing purchase, preorder, and sale records in a transaction ledger.

  • Track key performance indicators: acquisition cost, estimated resale revenue, gross profit potential, unit count, and average margin.
  • Compare product performance: review gross profit by model and compare purchase costs against target resale values over time.
  • Explore transactions: filter by date, product model, storage, color, and status, then scroll the ledger independently.
  • Apply data analysis techniques: organize records with SQL aggregations and data-quality checks, and use Excel-style lookups, PivotTables, and conditional formatting.
