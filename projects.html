Purchase & Sales & Inventory Analytics

Interactive dashboard

This project brings purchase activity, target resale revenue, gross profit, and inventory status into one reporting view. The dashboard helps compare product models, storage capacities, colors, acquisition costs, and estimated resale margins while keeping purchase, preorder, and sale records organized in a transaction ledger.

  • Track key performance indicators: acquisition cost, estimated resale revenue, gross profit potential, unit count, and average margin.
  • Compare product performance: review gross profit by model and compare purchase costs against target resale values over time.
  • Explore transactions: filter by date, product model, storage, color, and status, then scroll the ledger independently to review more records.
  • Apply data analysis techniques: organize records with SQL aggregations and data-quality checks, and use Excel-style lookups, PivotTables, and conditional formatting to support inventory review.
