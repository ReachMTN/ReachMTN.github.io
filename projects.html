Checkout, Sales Performance & Inventory Analytics

Interactive Demo

A combined analytics project that brings checkout spending, transaction activity, sales performance, and inventory priorities into one reporting experience. The dashboard highlights headline KPIs, spending trends, product mix, and recent checkout records, while the companion analysis framework supports sales and inventory review.

  • Monitor total spend, savings, checkout volume, and failed checkouts through KPI cards.
  • Compare product variants and review daily spending trends to spot changes in purchasing activity.
  • Use Excel-style analysis concepts—PivotTables, formulas, lookups, and conditional formatting—to summarize sales and inventory performance.
  • Use SQL joins, aggregations, and data-quality checks to organize records and support reliable reporting.
  • Use the combined reporting view to help identify product trends and potential inventory follow-up priorities.
