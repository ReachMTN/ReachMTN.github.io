Purchase & Sales & Inventory Analytics

This project analyzes purchase activity, target resale revenue, gross profit, and inventory status using Power BI-style reporting and SQL. It compares product models, storage capacities, colors, acquisition costs, and estimated resale margins while organizing purchase, preorder, and sale records in a transaction ledger.

  • Track key performance indicators: acquisition cost, target resale revenue, gross profit potential, unit count, and average margin.
  • Compare product performance: review gross profit by model and purchase costs against target resale values over time.
  • Explore transactions: filter by date, model, storage, color, and status, with an independently scrollable ledger.
  • Apply analytics techniques: SQL aggregations, data-quality checks, KPI design, and inventory review.
