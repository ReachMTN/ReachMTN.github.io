Checkout, Sales Performance & Inventory Analytics

Interactive Demo

An interactive resale analytics project modeling iPhone 18 Pro Max launch-day purchases on September 18, 2026, at published U.S. MSRP by storage tier and target resale profit near $180 per unit. It also tracks iPhone Duo 256GB preorders placed October 16, 2026, at $1,999 each, separate from fulfilled inventory and completed sales.

  • Compare purchase cost, estimated resale revenue, gross profit, and average margin per unit.
  • Filter by product model, storage capacity, color, date range, and Sold vs. In Stock status.
  • Analyze iPhone 18 Pro Max storage tiers from 256GB to 2TB and other models to compare product mix.
  • Use SQL aggregations and data-quality checks to organize inventory and support reliable reporting.
  • Apply Excel-style PivotTables, lookups, and conditional formatting to support inventory and sales review.
